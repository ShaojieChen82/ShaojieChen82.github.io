CHP · DER · Systems Integration

E8kW Micro-CHP System Development

Developing a residential micro combined heat and power system requires the mechanical, electrical, thermal, and control subsystems to behave as one product rather than independent components.

Problem

Integrate an engine-generator platform with power conversion, energy storage, heat recovery, hot-water loads, and residential operating logic while retaining practical testability and serviceability.

What I owned / contributed

  • Integrated system thinking across engine-generator hardware, PMG/rectification, inverter, battery, sensors, and thermal interfaces.
  • Developed operating logic around electrical demand, battery SOC windows, thermal deadbands, hot-water demand, and real residential behavior.
  • Planned and executed electrical/thermal validation needed to move architecture decisions from assumptions to measured behavior.
  • Translated prototype findings into engineering actions for internal teams and suppliers.

Power Electronics · BESS · Validation

Hybrid Inverter / BESS Validation

Power-path behavior matters at the transitions: grid bypass, backup operation, generator input, battery charging/discharging, and transfer events.

Problem

Determine how candidate inverter and battery architectures actually behave under realistic source and load conditions so system decisions are based on measured performance.

What I built / validated

  • Built instrumented AC/DC bench setups for power-path, source-transfer, charging, and load-response validation.
  • Measured and reviewed bypass behavior, ATS/transfer timing, DC charging behavior, efficiency, ripple, and waveform quality.
  • Used Dewesoft and voltage/current instrumentation to capture transient and steady-state electrical behavior.
  • Converted measured results into practical recommendations for generator, inverter, battery, and control integration.

A hybrid-energy product cannot be developed efficiently if every test depends on manually creating a different house load, hot-water condition, or operator sequence.

Problem

Create repeatable electrical and thermal load conditions for validating generator, inverter, battery, controls, domestic-hot-water behavior, and system-level energy management.

What I built

  • Developed repeatable electrical and thermal load-emulation concepts and hardware for system-level testing.
  • Integrated PLC/Modbus, Arduino/ESP32-class controls, sensors, motorized loads, and custom electronics where appropriate.
  • Automated test behavior so the same operating scenario can be reproduced and compared across hardware or software revisions.
  • Applied limits, interlocks, and instrumentation with a practical test-cell safety mindset.

Active Aero · Mechanical Design · Fabrication

C7 Corvette Active Aero / DRS Development Platform

This is the master vehicle-development project: I took an active-aero idea through rapid prototypes, composite fabrication, aluminum hardware, actuation, front/rear aero integration, controls, on-car testing, and track use.

Engineering objective

Develop a functional active-aero system for a C7 Corvette Grand Sport that can trade drag and downforce by vehicle state while surviving real packaging, stiffness, actuation, and track constraints.

What I designed and built

  • Iterated rear-wing geometry, brackets, mounting, and packaging with 3D-printed fit checks before committing to final hardware.
  • Fabricated composite wing structures using printed cores, carbon cloth, vacuum-bagging steps, post-processing, and clear finishing.
  • Transitioned prototype brackets and posts to aluminum hardware and validated fitment on the vehicle.
  • Designed front-aero concepts including movable splitter/ramp states for high-downforce versus low-drag operation.
  • Integrated powered actuation and position feedback so aero state can be commanded manually or automatically.
  • Validated hardware on the car, at public demonstrations, and through track-development use rather than stopping at CAD or a bench prototype.

Embedded Controls · CAN · PCB · iOS HMI

ESP32 / CAN Active-Aero Controller and Driver HMI

The aero hardware is controlled as a vehicle system, not as isolated motors. Vehicle CAN signals, actuator feedback, manual commands, automatic state logic, safety behavior, and a driver-facing HMI all participate.

Control problem

Turn vehicle state and driver intent into deterministic front/rear aero commands while retaining direct manual control, position awareness, debugging access, and safe stop behavior.

What I developed

  • Built ESP32 + MCP2515 CAN integration and decoded vehicle signals including throttle, brake, wheel speed, and steering-wheel button inputs.
  • Implemented closed-loop rear-actuator positioning, filtered feedback, deadband logic, watchdog behavior, STOP priority, and manual override.
  • Developed AUTO behavior around driver/vehicle inputs and MANUAL behavior for direct front and rear positioning.
  • Moved from breadboard/prototype electronics toward a purpose-built PCB architecture documented with 2D and 3D board design evidence.
  • Integrated an iOS HMI for mode control, front/rear manual commands, precision positioning, engineering/debug information, and live system feedback.
  • Validated control through on-car phone operation and physical/remote command testing.

Vehicle Development · Track Data · Correlation

Track-Test and Aero / Chassis Correlation

Aero and control changes only matter if the complete car behaves better. Track use closes the loop between mechanical design, control behavior, suspension travel, driver feedback, and data.

Development objective

Use real vehicle data and repeated track experience to determine whether aero, chassis, and control changes are helping the car and to identify the next engineering problem worth solving.

How I validate and iterate

  • Run the C7 at Gingerman and review PDR/Pi Toolbox data after sessions rather than relying only on subjective feel.
  • Use suspension-travel behavior and observed front travel limits to reason about aero balance, ride-height sensitivity, and high-speed understeer.
  • Compare straight-line behavior and aero states to verify that drag-reduction concepts do not create unintended speed penalties.
  • Perform real trackside troubleshooting, brake/service work, setup checks, and hardware iteration as part of the development cycle.
  • Feed track findings back into front/rear aero geometry, control logic, sensing, and future instrumentation priorities.

Instrumentation · Thermal Imaging · Distributed Sensing

Suspension, Tire, and Vehicle Instrumentation Platform

The next level of vehicle development is better observability: suspension motion, tire thermal behavior, loads, aero state, and synchronized driver/system information.

Instrumentation objective

Build a scalable sensor platform that produces useful vehicle-state information without turning the car into an unmaintainable lab harness.

Current development

  • Developing suspension-travel and wheel-side sensing concepts for setup and aero-correlation work.
  • Exploring strain-gauge / HX711 load measurement for structural or suspension-related force measurement.
  • Developing tire thermal imaging using distributed ESP32 sensor nodes and live camera/thermal feeds.
  • Building an iOS-facing visualization path so instrumentation becomes usable in the garage, during testing, and during development—not just raw logged data.
