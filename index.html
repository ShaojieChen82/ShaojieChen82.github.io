01 · PRODUCT DEVELOPMENT

E8kW Micro-CHP: Hardware, Controls & Integration

Bring the engine-generator, inverter, battery, thermal hardware and operating controls together as one residential energy system.

The goal

A residential CHP platform needs its mechanical, electrical, thermal and control interfaces to work together.

My contribution

  • Developed Arduino logic that reads battery SOC from the inverter over Modbus and commands the engine ECU to increase, hold or reduce throttle opening.
  • Designed, 3D printed and assembled exhaust-silencer prototypes; developed generator-cooling parts and a stator mockup for in-machine fit checks.
  • Worked on long-reach spark-plug development for the 8 kW opposed-piston engine, alongside broader system integration and test work.

How I validated

Used control-signal debugging, prototype fit checks and engine dyno testing to support development and integration.

What I delivered

Control logic and physical prototypes that supported integration and validation of the E8kW platform.

  1. Inverter battery SOC
  2. Arduino · Modbus logic
  3. ECU · more / hold / less throttle

02 · TEST INFRASTRUCTURE

Test Platforms, Dyno & Load Emulation

Build the equipment and reference data needed to evaluate an engine or an integrated energy system.

The goal

Give in-house engine development a complete benchmark platform and practical, repeatable test infrastructure.

My contribution

  • Starting with a supplied bare Kawasaki engine and PMG, independently designed and assembled the base, mechanical connection and electrical controls.
  • Designed, machined and assembled an AC dynamometer for engine-development testing.
  • Developed thermal-side load-emulation hardware for mCHP testing, alongside the electrical-load and automated-test work.

How I validated

Led benchmark testing and data collection covering generation efficiency, noise, power quality, ripple and other generator-performance parameters.

What I delivered

A complete benchmark test platform and reference data to inform the company’s in-house engine development.

03 · MEASUREMENT & DEBUGGING

Data Acquisition & Custom Measurement Hardware

Make prototype behavior observable, then use the measurements to guide the next engineering decision.

The goal

Capture useful electrical and system behavior when standard interfaces alone are not enough.

My contribution

  • Built Dewesoft-based measurement workflows for electrical and system-level testing.
  • Designed and assembled custom signal-conditioning / amplifier hardware to interface measurement signals with the DAQ.
  • Used synchronized waveforms to investigate transitions, compare configurations and verify engineering changes.

How I validated

Paired instrumentation with bench and system tests so measured behavior could be reviewed alongside the operating condition.

What I delivered

Measurement workflows and custom interfaces that made prototypes easier to investigate and supported integration decisions.

04 · POWER-SYSTEM VALIDATION

Hybrid Inverter & BESS Validation

Test the power paths and transitions that determine how a generator, inverter and battery work together.

The goal

Evaluate source transfer, bypass, charging and load response in a real generator–inverter–battery architecture.

My contribution

  • Built instrumented AC/DC bench setups for generator input, charging, bypass and transfer events.
  • Measured transfer timing, waveform quality, ripple, efficiency, charging behavior and power-path response using Dewesoft and electrical instrumentation.

How I validated

Compared measured operating behavior across test conditions and configurations.

What I delivered

Practical architecture recommendations for generator, inverter, battery and control integration.

01 · BUILD · MECHANICAL DESIGN · FABRICATION

C7 Active Aero Design & Fabrication

Composite structures, aluminum hardware and vehicle packaging, followed by an actuator experiment that revealed a speed-versus-holding tradeoff.

Built hardware · Servo bench + road evaluation

What I built

  • Used 3D-printed prototypes to check wing geometry, brackets and vehicle packaging before fabricating carbon-composite structures and aluminum hardware.
  • Developed movable front aero and integrated actuation and feedback into the vehicle.

What I tested

I evaluated servo motors as an alternative to linear actuators. The servo setup delivered much faster motion and response in bench testing.

What road testing revealed

The tested servo setup lacked self-locking capability and sufficient holding torque to resist aerodynamic loading. A higher-torque servo setup or faster linear actuator remains a candidate for further evaluation.

02 · CONTROL · EMBEDDED SYSTEMS · DRIVER HMI

CAN / ESP32 Control: From Design to V2 Hardware

A modular V2 controller prototype, designed, hand-assembled, integrated and debugged by me.

V2 prototype · Bench + road tested

My contribution

  • Designed the PCB, had it fabricated by JLC, then soldered, integrated and debugged the prototype myself.
  • Integrated ESP32 + MCP2515 CAN with throttle, brake, wheel-speed and steering-wheel inputs.
  • Implemented position feedback, deadband, watchdog behavior, STOP priority and automatic / manual modes.

Development approach

V2 uses a custom board to bring proven modules together as a step toward a more fully integrated PCB.

How I validated it

The V2 controller underwent bench and road testing. The earlier V1 active-aero system was used on track; V2 has not yet been track-tested.

03 · MEASURE · INSTRUMENTATION · THERMAL SENSING

Tire-Thermal Sensing & Instrumentation

Bench prototypes and architecture refinement toward infrared tire-temperature sensing with an iPhone or iPad HMI.

Thermal sensing · Bench prototype

What I prototyped

Bench-tested a four-node concept combining visible cameras and infrared thermal sensing, with an ESP32-P4 serving as the HMI.

How the direction evolved

I later narrowed the scope to infrared-only tire-temperature sensing with an iPhone or iPad HMI. The P4 and visible-camera media document the earlier architecture.

Development stage

Thermal sensing remains at the bench-testing stage. Broader instrumentation work includes suspension-travel sensing and strain / HX711 load-measurement concepts.

04 · VALIDATE · TRACK DEVELOPMENT · ITERATION

Track Validation & Vehicle Development

Track use of the V1 active-aero system connects design and setup decisions with data review, driver feedback and hardware iteration.

V1 active aero · Track tested

How I validate and iterate

  • Run the C7 at Gingerman and Grattan and review PDR / Pi Toolbox data after sessions.
  • Use suspension-travel behavior and observed limits to investigate aero balance, ride-height sensitivity and high-speed understeer.
  • Perform trackside troubleshooting, service work and setup checks as part of the development loop.

Version context

The track-tested active-aero configuration is V1. V2 controller testing covers bench and road use; the thermal-sensing project remains at the bench stage.
